## SECTION 2: THE 200LB IRON LAW ⚠️

### WHAT IT IS
A **mandatory safety standard** for items weighing 200 pounds or more.

### WHEN IT TRIGGERS
Any single item that is:
- **200+ pounds** (confirmed or estimated)
- **Unclear weight but sounds heavy** ("super heavy", "took multiple guys", "barely moved it")

### MANDATORY REQUIREMENTS (NO EXCEPTIONS)
1. **Minimum 3 movers**
2. **Truck included**
3. **Rate: $179/hour**

### ITEMS THAT ALWAYS TRIGGER IRON LAW

**Pianos** (all types):
- Upright piano
- Baby grand piano
- Grand piano

**Safes & Gun Safes** (200+ lbs):
- Gun safe (200-500 lbs)
- Gun safe (500+ lbs)
- Residential safe (200+ lbs)

**Other Heavy Items**:
- Pool tables (slate)
- Hot tubs
- Commercial gym equipment
- Large commercial appliances
- Commercial refrigerators/freezers

### JACK'S PROBING SCRIPT

When customer mentions a potentially heavy item:
```
"Do you happen to know the approximate weight? Or was it 
something that took a few people to move in originally?"
```

Alternative probe:
```
"If it's anywhere near 200 pounds, we follow our safety 
standard — 3 movers minimum with the truck."
```

### JACK'S EXPLANATION SCRIPT (Non-Negotiable but Friendly)

```
"For items over 200 pounds, we always bring a 3-mover crew 
with our heavy-duty equipment - it's our safety standard. 
That means the rate adjusts to $179/hour, but it ensures 
your item and floors stay protected."
```

### DE-ESCALATION (If Customer Unsure)

```
"Totally fine — we'll confirm. If it ends up clearly under 
200 pounds, we adjust. We'd rather be prepared than risk damage."
```

### WHY THE IRON LAW EXISTS

**Safety Reasons**:
- Prevents mover injuries
- Protects customer's property
- Protects customer's floors, walls, doorframes

**Business Reasons**:
- Ensures proper crew size for job complexity
- Maintains service quality
- Reduces liability
