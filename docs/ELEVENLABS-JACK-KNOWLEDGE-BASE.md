# SECTION 4: THE 200 POUND IRON LAW (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

## What It Is
A mandatory safety standard for items weighing 200 pounds or more. This rule protects customers, their property, and our movers.

## When It Triggers
Any single item that is 200 pounds or more (confirmed or estimated). Any item described as "super heavy", "took multiple guys", or "barely moved it". Any unclear weight that sounds heavy.

## Mandatory Requirements (NO EXCEPTIONS)
1. Minimum 3 movers
2. Truck included
3. Rate: $179 per hour

## Items That ALWAYS Trigger Iron Law
Pianos: All upright pianos. All baby grand pianos. All grand pianos.
Safes: Gun safes 200 to 500 pounds. Gun safes 500 pounds and over. Residential safes 200 pounds and over.
Other Heavy Items: Pool tables with slate. Hot tubs. Commercial gym equipment. Large commercial appliances. Commercial refrigerators and freezers.

## Jack's Probing Script
"Do you happen to know the approximate weight? Or was it something that took a few people to move in originally?"

Alternative probe: "If it's anywhere near 200 pounds, we follow our safety standard — 3 movers minimum with the truck."

## Jack's Explanation Script
"For items over 200 pounds, we always bring a 3-mover crew with our heavy-duty equipment - it's our safety standard. That means the rate adjusts to $179 per hour, but it ensures your item and floors stay protected."

## De-escalation Script (If Customer Is Unsure)
"Totally fine — we'll confirm. If it ends up clearly under 200 pounds, we adjust. We'd rather be prepared than risk damage."

## Why The Iron Law Exists
Safety Reasons: Prevents mover injuries. Protects customer's property. Protects floors, walls, and doorframes.
Business Reasons: Ensures proper crew size for job complexity. Maintains service quality. Reduces liability.
